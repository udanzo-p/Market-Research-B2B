    What moved from "unverified" to evidence

    • Supplier tie-ups. Earlier: unknown for every partner. Now: 27 developer specs and 11 hotel references read. Most developer specs are open ("Jaquar or equivalent"); hotel references cluster on Jaquar/Artize, TOTO, GROHE and Duravit. See Partners.
    • Polymer in projects. No developer or hotel spec names PTMT. Incumbents place polymer at the value end (Cera Polipluz, Truflo by Hindware, Prince Duratap). This is why NuFlow's finish-led, mid-premium hybrid is the right B2B product, and why the spec pack must carry IS marks and a brass-equivalent warranty.
    • Fittings spend per bathroom. Earlier: one assumption. Now anchored to published retail tiers (economy ₹9–15k, standard ₹22–35k, luxury ₹55k+) and the plan's project kits (₹4,268 to ₹11,445 realised).
    • Developer-fitted share. Apartments ship with CP fittings as standard; villas and plots do not. Raised from 60% to 85% of launches, tagged assumed.
    • BirlaNu's base. 16 named clients, 5,937 dealers, 90.7% of sales through them, 64,921 plumber and contractor meets in FY26. Warm routes to DLF, M3M, Mahindra Lifespaces, Casagrand, Raheja, Appaswamy and four EPC contractors.

    Still open: whether luxury hotel brand standards allow polymer (no standard found either way), the exact project discount depth (trade lore says 40–60% off MRP), and the opening status of Radisson's two Hyderabad hotels.

    So what for the institutional entry

    1. Two markets, two motions. Self-build and replacement (60%+ of faucet demand) are reached through dealers and plumbers: that is the Reach model. Organised projects (≈40% of organised faucet sales, ₹4,800 Cr) are reached through specification: that is this plan. Projects feed dealers with references; dealers service what projects install.
    2. The bathroom count is set by the price band. Using 1.5 bathrooms below ₹40 L, 2 at ₹40–80 L, 2.5 at ₹80 L–1.5 Cr and 3 above, the Q3 2026 launch mix gives 2.4 bathrooms per home. Premiumisation adds bathrooms faster than it adds homes.
    3. Affordable is open but ISI-led. Signature Global's HRERA specs say only "ISI marked". PMAY partnership housing and affordable towers are where a certified polymer CORE kit competes on price without a brand fight.
    4. Tier 2 waits for the dealer network. Projects there average 81 units and sit with local architects. They are a Year 3+ project market, reached first through BirlaNu's dealers and the plumber scheme.

    The "behind-the-wall plus on-the-wall" offer

    The plan's riskiest assumption is seven developer phases in Year 1. The argument for it is a single-supplier plumbing spec:

    • One spec. CPVC and uPVC pipes, valves, fittings and NuFlow CP kits specified together, with one warranty and one service hotline.
    • One approval. The developer's existing approved-vendor status for BirlaNu pipes shortens the "or equivalent" approval for the faucet.
    • One site team. BirlaNu's plumber and contractor meets (64,921 in FY26) become the installer training for kits.
    • One invoice. Project procurement prefers fewer vendors; MEP contractors prefer fewer deliveries.

    Discipline: approach the warm accounts only with the spec pack ready (test reports, IS marks, warranty, price book). A warm door opened too early is a cold door later.

    Go-to-market playbook · deck-ready

    Spec-in, land, expand, pull through.

    The institutional motion in the language of the main deck: ideal customer profile, positioning, value proposition by persona, the sales motion, offer architecture, demand generation, objection handling, gates and KPIs, and the sequence.

    Positioning statement

    For developers and hotel owners who specify mid-premium bathrooms, NuFlow is the fittings system that gives a brass-brand finish and warranty at polymer-hybrid economics, from the company already behind their walls.

    Claim discipline from the plan: "tested against stated water conditions", never "hard-water proof"; no blanket "lead-free".

    Reasons to believe

    • Quarter-turn ceramic cartridges tested to 200,000 cycles; IS 9763 / IS 8931 mapping; IS 17650-2 water efficiency.
    • QR product passport with test results; front-serviceable modules; 5-year parts-only warranty.
    • Brass is 48.7% of India's faucet material and rose about 40% in a year; the polymer-hybrid body de-risks the developer's BOQ.
    • One plumbing spec with BirlaNu pipes, valves and chemicals: one vendor, one warranty, one site team.

    Value proposition by persona

    Who decides, what they are judged on, and what NuFlow brings. Roles follow the professional directions document.

    Sales motion: spec-in → land → expand → pull through

    1Spec-in

    Get on the approved list

    • Ask every target for its approved-vendor process and current bathware supplier (first meeting, every time).
    • Submit the spec pack: test reports, IS marks, warranty, CAD/BIM, price book. Pass the "or equivalent" clause.
    • Architect-specification manager logs each spec written (target: 40 by Mar 2028).
    2Land

    One phase, one sample flat

    • Pilot a tower or phase with a sample flat the developer's sales team uses.
    • Install with trained fitters; record callbacks, cost per bathroom and installer feedback.
    • PO with 20% advance; milestone billing; receivables ≤ 60 days.
    3Expand

    Framework across the developer's book

    • Convert the pilot into a framework MoU across upcoming phases (GROHE did this with Gaurs for 40,000 NCR homes in July 2026).
    • Bundle with BirlaNu pipes and chemicals on the next project's BOQ.
    • Repeat orders from the same developer are the KPI that matters.
    4Pull through

    Projects feed retail

    • Each installed flat is a demonstration for the buyer's next renovation and for the dealer cluster around it.
    • QR-verified installs route spares, HUE inserts and warranty claims to the brand site.
    • Studios in the same cluster carry the hero 8 SKUs seen in the show flat.
